## Targeting databases (method language, not endorsements)

Construction / FM: Companies House SIC filters, Barbour ABI / Glenigan-style project intel, LinkedIn Sales Nav by role (QS, PM, Head of Estates), framework portals.  
Legal: SRA firm lists, Law Society Find a Solicitor, Clio/LEAP practice segments, Google Ads practice keywords.  
Procurement: Apollo / LinkedIn for Head of Procurement titles, Companies House for mid-market buyers, Canopy ICP sectors.  
Wealth / insurance: FCA register segments, LinkedIn IFAs / brokers, Apollo for BDM roles.  
Medical: CQC register, clinic directories, LegitScript where ads require it.
